# CountYourBite — Privacy Policy **Effective Date:** October 22, 2026 CountYourBite is a food diary and nutrition-tracking application designed to help users record meals, calories and macronutrients. ### Information Stored on Your Device CountYourBite stores food diary entries, custom foods, recipes and nutrition goals locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData technology. CountYourBite does not require users to create an account. ### Online Food Searches When you choose to search for food online, your search terms are sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve nutritional information. Open Food Facts may process technical information, such as your IP address, according to its own privacy practices. Your personal food diary, saved recipes and nutrition goals are not sent to Open Food Facts. ### Analytics and Advertising CountYourBite Version 1.0 does not include advertising, advertising trackers or third-party analytics SDKs. ### Data Retention and Deletion Your nutrition records remain on your device unless you remove them through the app or delete the app and its locally stored data. ### Children's Privacy CountYourBite is intended for general nutrition tracking and does not knowingly solicit personal information from children. ### Nutrition Disclaimer Nutritional information is provided for general informational purposes. Calorie and nutrient values are estimates and do not constitute medical advice. ### Changes to This Policy This policy may be updated as CountYourBite develops. Changes will be published on this page. ### Contact For privacy questions or support, please use the CountYourBite support page once published.
